About Today’s Dish

A simple daily food trend: eat today’s dish.
Today’s Dishes Cook with what I have About

What is this?

Today’s Dish is built for one job: remove the “what should we eat?” decision. Every day the site shows a breakfast, lunch, and dinner idea — and that’s it.

The dream is to make a fun little trend where people visit the site, pick today’s dish, and just eat.

Why I made it

The daily question “what’s for dinner?” never ends. I got tired of browsing recipe lists that still don’t help you decide… so I built something that decides for you.

How to use it

  • Step 1: Open Today’s Dishes and check today’s picks.
  • Step 2: Pick one and cook it — no second guessing.
  • Step 3: If you’re not feeling today’s picks, use Cook with what I have to get ideas based on ingredients you already have.
New ideas. One day at a time.
