What is this?

Today's Dish is built for one job: remove the “what should we eat?” decision. Every day, the site shows a breakfast, lunch, and dinner idea — and that’s it.

The goal is to make a fun, viral habit where people visit the site, pick today’s dish, and just eat.

Why I made it

This started because the daily question “what’s for dinner?” never ends. I got bored of browsing lists of recipes that still don’t help you decide. So I built something that simply decides for you.

How to use it

  • Step 1: Open the Home page and see today’s dishes.
  • Step 2: If you like one, cook it — no second guessing.
  • Step 3: If you don’t like today’s picks, use Cook with what I have to find other ideas based on your ingredients.

Future plans (optional)

Right now, this project is about building a daily habit and making it fun to share. In future, if lots of people use it, I might explore simple ways to support it (e.g., ads, affiliate links, partnerships), but the main goal will always be: come here, get today’s dish, and eat.
