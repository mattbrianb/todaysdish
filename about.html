About Today's Dish

A simple daily food trend: eat today’s dish.
Home About

What is this?

Today's Dish is built for one job: remove the “what should we eat?” decision. Every day the site shows a breakfast, lunch, and dinner idea — and that’s it.

I want this to become a fun little trend where people visit the site, pick today’s dish, and just eat.

Why I made it

This started because the daily question “what’s for dinner?” never ends. I got tired of browsing recipe lists that still don’t help you decide… so I built something that decides for you.

How to use it

  • Step 1: Open Home and check today’s dishes.
  • Step 2: Pick one and cook it — no second guessing.
  • Step 3: If you’re not feeling today’s picks, use Cook with what I have for other ideas based on your ingredients.

Future plans (optional)

Right now this is purely about building a daily habit and making it fun to share. If it grows, I might explore simple ways to support it (ads/affiliate links/partnerships), but the main goal stays the same: come here, get today’s dish, and eat.

Some dietary filters are labeled “best effort” when the data can’t guarantee a perfect match.

New ideas. One day at a time.
